1. Delivery Performance

Suppliers are expected to deliver orders by the agreed delivery date specified in the purchase or logistics agreement.

A delivery is considered late when the actual delivery date is later than the agreed delivery date.

Repeated late deliveries may trigger a supplier performance review.

2. Delivery Confirmation

Suppliers should provide shipment and delivery confirmation information when goods are dispatched and delivered.

The following information should be available where applicable:

* Order reference
* Shipment reference
* Dispatch date
* Expected delivery date
* Actual delivery date
* Carrier information

3. Supplier Performance Review

Supplier delivery performance may be reviewed using measures such as:

* On-time delivery rate
* Late delivery rate
* Average delivery delay
* Number of delayed shipments

Performance thresholds and corrective actions should be defined in the applicable supplier agreement.

4. Escalation

If a supplier repeatedly fails to meet agreed delivery dates, the logistics team may request a corrective action plan.

The corrective action plan should identify the cause of the delays and proposed actions to improve future delivery performance.
