1. Purpose

This document provides simplified guidance on selected Incoterms used in international logistics.

This document is intended for the portfolio project and is not a substitute for an official Incoterms publication or contractual advice.

2. EXW - Ex Works

Under EXW, the seller generally makes the goods available at the seller's premises or another agreed location.

The buyer generally takes responsibility for arranging transportation from the agreed location.

3. FCA - Free Carrier

Under FCA, the seller delivers the goods to the carrier or another party nominated by the buyer at the agreed location.

The specific delivery point should be clearly defined because responsibilities can depend on the agreed location.

4. DAP - Delivered At Place

Under DAP, the seller is generally responsible for arranging transportation to the named destination.

The goods are made available to the buyer at the destination, ready for unloading.

5. DDP - Delivered Duty Paid

Under DDP, the seller generally takes responsibility for transporting the goods to the named destination and handling import formalities and applicable duties.

DDP therefore places substantial responsibility on the seller.

6. Logistics Consideration

When analyzing a shipment, the applicable Incoterm should be considered together with the agreed delivery location, carrier arrangement, transport responsibilities, and contractual terms.
